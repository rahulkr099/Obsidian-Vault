# Module 11 — Lesson 11.2: Designing Multi-Repository Workflows ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> **Goal:** Learn how a Service coordinates multiple repositories to complete one business action.

Think of the **Service** as the **conductor of an orchestra**.

Each repository has one job, and the service tells them **when** to perform it.

---

# Example 1 — Blog App

Requirement:

> User publishes a post.

Workflow:

```text
PostService

↓

PostRepository
(Update post)

↓

ActivityRepository
(Create activity)

↓

NotificationRepository
(Create notification)
```

One business action.

Three repositories.

---

# Example 2 — E-commerce

Requirement:

> Customer places an order.

Workflow:

```text
OrderService

↓

OrderRepository
(Create order)

↓

InventoryRepository
(Reduce stock)

↓

PaymentRepository
(Create payment)
```

Again:

One service.

Multiple repositories.

---

# Example 3 — Banking

Requirement:

> Transfer money.

Workflow:

```text
TransferService

↓

AccountRepository
(Withdraw)

↓

AccountRepository
(Deposit)

↓

TransactionRepository
(Save history)
```

The service coordinates the entire process.

---

# The Service is the Orchestrator

Think of it like this:

```text
Controller

↓

Service
(Decision Maker)

↓

Repository A

Repository B

Repository C
```

Repositories don't know about each other.

Only the service knows the complete workflow.

---

# Common Mistake

❌ Bad

```text
OrderRepository

↓

InventoryRepository

↓

PaymentRepository
```

Now repositories are tightly coupled.

Instead:

```text
OrderService

↓

OrderRepository

InventoryRepository

PaymentRepository
```

The service coordinates everything.

---

# Best Practices

✅ One repository = One data source.

✅ One service = One business workflow.

✅ Keep orchestration inside the Service Layer.

---

# Interview Question

**Q:** What does it mean for a service to orchestrate multiple repositories?

**Good Answer:**

> It means the service coordinates multiple repositories to complete one business workflow. Each repository performs its own data access, while the service controls the sequence and business rules.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Services orchestrate workflows.
    
- Repositories handle only their own data.
    
- Repositories should not depend on each other.
    
- One business action may involve many repositories.
