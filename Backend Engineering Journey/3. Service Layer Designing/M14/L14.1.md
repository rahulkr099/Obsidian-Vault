# Lesson 14.1 — When Should One Service Call Another?

> **Goal:** Learn when it's appropriate for one service to use another service.

---

# Example — Blog App

Requirement:

> User publishes a post.

Workflow:

```text
PostService

↓

Update Post

↓

NotificationService

↓

EmailService
```

`PostService` owns the workflow, but it **delegates** notifications to the appropriate services.

---

# Another Example

Requirement:

> User registers.

Workflow:

```text
AuthService

↓

Create User

↓

EmailService

↓

Send Verification Email
```

`AuthService` doesn't know how to send emails.

It asks `EmailService` to do that.

---

# Good Principle

Each service should ask:

> **"Can another service do this better?"**

If yes:

```text
Call that service
```

Don't duplicate its logic.

---

# Bad Example

❌ `PostService` sends emails directly.

```text
PostService

↓

SMTP Logic

↓

Email Templates

↓

Send Email
```

Now email logic is duplicated across the application.

---

# Better Design

```text
PostService

↓

EmailService

↓

Send Email
```

Each service has one responsibility.

---

# Best Practices

✅ Call another service only when it owns that responsibility.

✅ Delegate specialized work.

✅ Keep business workflows in the calling service.

---

# Interview Question

**Q:** When should one service call another?

**Good Answer:**

> A service should call another service when the second service owns a separate business responsibility, such as sending emails or notifications. This avoids duplication and keeps responsibilities clearly separated.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Services can collaborate.
    
- Delegate specialized responsibilities.
    
- Avoid duplicating logic.
    
- Keep the workflow in the calling service.
