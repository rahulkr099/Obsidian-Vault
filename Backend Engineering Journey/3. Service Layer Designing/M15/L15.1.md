# Lesson 15.1 — Why Business Logic Should Be Reusable

> **Goal:** Learn why duplicated business logic is a problem.

---

# The Problem

Suppose many services check ownership.

```text
PostService

↓

Check Owner

----------------

CommentService

↓

Check Owner

----------------

TodoService

↓

Check Owner
```

Now the same rule exists in three places.

---

# The Risk

Later the rule changes.

Example:

Old rule:

```text
Owner only
```

New rule:

```text
Owner

OR

Admin
```

Now you must update every service.

It's easy to miss one.

---

# Better Design

Extract the shared rule.

```text
PermissionService

↓

canEdit()

↓

canDelete()

↓

canPublish()
```

Now every service reuses the same logic.

---

# Example

```text
PostService

↓

PermissionService

↓

PostRepository
```

```text
CommentService

↓

PermissionService

↓

CommentRepository
```

The rule is written once.

---

# Benefits

✅ No duplicated business rules

✅ Easier maintenance

✅ Consistent behavior

✅ Easier testing

---

# Interview Question

**Q:** Why should business logic be reusable?

**Good Answer:**

> Reusable business logic avoids duplication, keeps behavior consistent across the application, and makes future changes easier because the rule only needs to be updated in one place.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Don't duplicate business rules.
    
- Extract shared logic.
    
- Reuse the same implementation across services.
    
- Keep business behavior consistent.
