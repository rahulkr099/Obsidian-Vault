# Module 15 — Lesson 15.4: Reusable Business Logic Best Practices ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> **Goal:** Learn the practical rules for reusing business logic without over-engineering.

This lesson summarizes Module 15.

---

# Rule 1 — Don't Repeat Business Rules

❌ Bad

```text
PostService

↓

Ownership Check

----------------

CommentService

↓

Ownership Check

----------------

TodoService

↓

Ownership Check
```

✅ Better

```text
PermissionService

↓

canEdit()

↓

canDelete()
```

Write the rule once. Reuse it everywhere.

---

# Rule 2 — Choose the Right Abstraction

Small reusable logic:

```text
Helper
```

Business workflow:

```text
Service
```

Don't create a service when a helper is enough.

---

# Rule 3 — Keep Responsibilities Clear

Example:

```text
EmailService

↓

Send Emails
```

Not:

```text
EmailService

↓

Send Emails

↓

Upload Files

↓

Generate PDFs
```

One responsibility per service.

---

# Rule 4 — Reuse, Don't Copy

If three services need the same logic:

```text
UserService

OrderService

PostService
```

Extract it into one reusable component instead of copying the code.

---

# Rule 5 — Keep Business Logic Out of Controllers

❌ Bad

```text
Controller

↓

Permission Check

↓

Discount Calculation

↓

Call Service
```

✅ Good

```text
Controller

↓

Service

↓

PermissionService

↓

PricingHelper
```

Controllers should coordinate requests, not implement business rules.

---

# Common Mistakes

### ❌ Copy-Paste Logic

The same rule exists in multiple services.

---

### ❌ Too Many Tiny Services

Don't create a new service for every 5-line function.

---

### ❌ Fat Controllers

Business rules belong in services, not controllers.

---

# Interview Questions

### Q1. Why should business logic be reusable?

**Answer:**

> Reusing business logic reduces duplication, keeps behavior consistent, and makes future changes easier because the rule exists in one place.

---

### Q2. Helper or Service?

**Answer:**

- **Helper** → Small, stateless reusable logic.
    
- **Service** → Business workflows or logic involving repositories/other services.
    

---

### Q3. Where should shared permission logic live?

**Answer:**

> In a reusable component such as a `PermissionService`, so multiple services can use the same authorization rules.

|Problem|Solution|
|---|---|

|Business logic in controllers|Move to services|

# Practice Exercise

Design the reusable logic for:

> **An e-commerce application where Orders, Cart, and Wishlist all need discount calculations.**

A clean design:

```text
OrderService
        │
CartService
        │
WishlistService
        │
        ▼
PricingHelper
        │
calculateDiscount()
```

Notice:

- `PricingHelper` contains the reusable discount calculation.
    
- All three services reuse it.
    
- The calculation is maintained in **one place**.
