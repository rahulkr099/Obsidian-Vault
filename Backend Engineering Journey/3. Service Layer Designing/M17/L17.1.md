# Lesson 17.1 — What is Service Composition?

> **Goal:** Understand how small services work together to solve one business problem.

---

# The Problem

Suppose `PostService` does everything.

```text
PostService

↓

Create Post

↓

Generate Slug

↓

Upload Image

↓

Index Search

↓

Send Notification
```

The service becomes too large.

---

# Better Design

Break responsibilities into smaller services.

```text
PostService

↓

SlugService

↓

StorageService

↓

SearchService

↓

NotificationService
```

Each service has one responsibility.

---

# Real Example

Requirement:

> User creates a blog post.

Workflow:

```text
PostService

↓

SlugService

↓

PostRepository

↓

SearchService

↓

NotificationService
```

Each service performs one job.

Together they complete one business workflow.

---

# Why Composition?

Instead of writing one large service:

```text
One Huge Service

↓

Hard to Maintain
```

Use:

```text
Many Small Services

↓

Easy to Maintain
```

---

# Benefits

✅ Easier testing

✅ Better code reuse

✅ Smaller services

✅ Easier maintenance

---

# Interview Question

**Q:** What is service composition?

**Good Answer:**

> Service composition is the practice of building a complex business workflow by coordinating multiple small, focused services. Each service has one responsibility, making the system modular and easier to maintain.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Compose small services.
    
- Don't build one huge service.
    
- Each service has one responsibility.
    
- The calling service coordinates the workflow.
