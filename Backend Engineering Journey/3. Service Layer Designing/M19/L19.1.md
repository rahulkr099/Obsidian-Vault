# Lesson 19.1 — Why Test the Service Layer?

> **Goal:** Understand why services are the primary target of unit tests.

---

# What Should Be Tested?

The Service Layer contains:

- Business rules
    
- Authorization
    
- Validation
    
- Workflows
    
- Transactions
    

These are the most important parts of your backend.

---

# What Shouldn't Be Tested Here?

The database itself.

Instead:

```text
PostService

↓

Mock Repository
```

Your goal is to test the **business logic**, not MongoDB or PostgreSQL.

---

# Example

Requirement:

> Publish a post.

Business rule:

```text
Already Published?

↓

Yes

↓

Throw ConflictError
```

This is what your unit test should verify.

---

# Why Not Test Controllers?

Controllers are usually thin.

```text
Read Request

↓

Call Service

↓

Return Response
```

Most business behavior lives in the service.

---

# Benefits

✅ Fast tests

✅ No real database

✅ Easy to find business logic bugs

✅ Safe refactoring

---

# Interview Question

**Q:** Why do we mainly unit test the Service Layer?

**Good Answer:**

> Because the Service Layer contains the application's business logic. Unit tests verify business rules in isolation by mocking repositories and external dependencies, making tests fast and reliable.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Test the **Service Layer**, not the database.
    
- Mock repositories and external services.
    
- Verify business rules and workflows.
    
- Thin controllers require fewer unit tests.
