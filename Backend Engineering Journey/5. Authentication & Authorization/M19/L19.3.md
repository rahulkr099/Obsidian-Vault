# What is Security Monitoring?

Security monitoring means continuously observing authentication and authorization events.

Example:

```text
Login

↓

Password Reset

↓

MFA Disabled

↓

Role Changed
```

Every important event can be monitored.

---

# Why Monitoring Matters

Suppose an attacker logs into Rahul's account.

Authentication succeeds.

Without monitoring:

```text
Attack

↓

No One Notices
```

With monitoring:

```text
Attack

↓

Alert

↓

Investigation

↓

Response
```

Monitoring helps detect problems quickly.

# Interview Questions

### Q1. Why is security monitoring important?

**Answer:**

It helps detect suspicious behavior, identify attacks early, and respond before significant damage occurs.

---

### Q2. What events should trigger security alerts?

**Answer:**

Examples include repeated failed logins, MFA being disabled, password changes, privilege changes, and suspicious login activity.

---

### Q3. What is incident response?

**Answer:**

Incident response is the structured process of detecting, investigating, containing, recovering from, and learning from security incidents.

---

### Q4. Why are audit logs important during an investigation?

**Answer:**

They provide a reliable history of important actions, helping investigators understand who performed an action, when it occurred, and from where.
