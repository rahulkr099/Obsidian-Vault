# Common Interview Questions

## 1. Authentication vs Authorization

**Answer:**

Authentication verifies **who the user is**.

Authorization determines **what the user is allowed to do**.

---

## 2. Why Use JWT?

**Answer:**

JWT enables stateless authentication, making it easier to scale applications because each server can verify tokens independently.

---

## 3. Why Access Token + Refresh Token?

**Answer:**

Access tokens are short-lived to reduce risk.

Refresh tokens allow users to stay logged in without repeatedly entering credentials.

---

## 4. Why HttpOnly Cookies?

**Answer:**

They prevent JavaScript from reading cookie values, helping reduce the impact of many XSS attacks.

---

## 5. Why Email Verification?

**Answer:**

To confirm ownership of the email address and reduce fake or mistyped accounts.

---

## 6. Why Password Hashing?

**Answer:**

Passwords should never be stored in plain text. Hashing protects them if the database is compromised.

---

## 7. Why RBAC?

**Answer:**

RBAC simplifies permission management by assigning permissions to roles instead of individual users.

---

## 8. Why MFA?

**Answer:**

MFA adds another authentication factor, reducing the impact of stolen passwords.

---

## 9. Why OAuth?

**Answer:**

OAuth lets users grant limited access through an identity provider without sharing their password with your application.

---

## 10. Why Audit Logs?

**Answer:**

Audit logs provide accountability, support investigations, and help detect suspicious activity.

# Common Beginner Mistakes

## ❌ Storing Passwords in Plain Text

Always hash passwords.

---

## ❌ Long-Lived Access Tokens

Keep access tokens short-lived.

---

## ❌ No Refresh Tokens

Avoid forcing users to log in repeatedly.

---

## ❌ No Authorization

Authentication alone doesn't protect resources.

---

## ❌ Ignoring Audit Logs

Important actions should always be traceable.

---

## ❌ No Monitoring

Security doesn't stop after deployment.

- Log important events.

- Plan for scaling from the beginning.

# Final Interview Questions

### Q1. What are the essential components of a production authentication system?

**Answer:**

Registration, email verification, password hashing, JWT, refresh tokens, session management, authorization, audit logging, monitoring, and security controls such as rate limiting and MFA where appropriate.

---

### Q2. Why is layered security important?

**Answer:**

Because no single security mechanism is perfect. Multiple independent layers reduce the impact if one defense fails.

---

### Q3. What is the biggest mistake beginners make?

**Answer:**

Focusing only on login while ignoring authorization, session management, monitoring, testing, and production security.

---

### Q4. What should you think about before implementing authentication?

**Answer:**

Understand the application's business requirements, security risks, user roles, and operational needs. The architecture should be driven by those requirements.
